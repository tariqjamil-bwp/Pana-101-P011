# Prompts Used — Project 3: The Books Don't Match

## Initial Prompt

```
I have 124,500 PKR in cash that I counted by hand — it's money collected
from 10 friends for a group wedding gift. But the digital payment records
I received are a mess: nicknames, typos, and unclear memos.

I need a Python reconciliation script that:

1. Takes my known correct total and expected contributions per person
2. Reads the messy digital records (sender, amount, memo)
3. Applies name-mapping rules to interpret ambiguous entries
4. Finds the gap between the known total and the records
5. Identifies duplicate payments, shortages, and unknown senders
6. Tells me who needs follow-up

The messy records include entries like "ali r.", "fati", "usman123",
"sarah" (typo for Sara), etc. Rules for interpretation are in rules.json.
```
