# Task 2 — Connect Gmail (Read-Only)

**What I did:** Connected my Gmail account to Claude.ai as a read-only Connector, then asked the AI to retrieve real email threads.

## App Connected

- **App:** Gmail (via Claude.ai Connectors directory)
- **Permission granted:** Read-only (search, read, and summarize emails — no send, delete, or modify access)
- **Setup path:** Customize → Connectors → "+" → Browse connectors → Gmail → Connect → Authorize via Google sign-in

## How I Verified

1. Connected Gmail via the standard OAuth flow
2. Asked the AI to find and summarize a recent email thread
3. Cross-checked the summary against the actual email in Gmail
4. Confirmed the AI could only read — no compose or delete actions were available

## One-Sentence Permission Note

*"I granted Claude read-only access to my Gmail — it can search and read my emails but cannot send, delete, or modify anything."*

## Result

The connector successfully retrieved real email data from my inbox. The AI identified the correct threads and produced accurate summaries without needing me to copy-paste anything.
