👤 About me

I'm an AI/ML Engineer based in Karachi, Pakistan, specializing in building production AI systems across Agentic AI, Computer Vision, and NLP. I lead TensorDot Solutions, with a specialty in guiding research projects for PhD scholars and developing AI solutions for diverse clients.

My work spans the full stack — from training custom vision models (YOLO, Mask R-CNN, Vision Transformers) and building LLM agents & RAG pipelines (LangChain, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, Google ADK) to deploying scalable pipelines for multi-model benchmarking. I'm the author of the AI in Aviation newsletter, and co-authored LinAlg-Bench (NeurIPS 2026) — a diagnostic benchmark for LLM mathematical reasoning. I'm passionate about bridging research and real-world impact, particularly in EdTech, aviation, and healthcare.
